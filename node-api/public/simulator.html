    UNDERSTAND THE NUMBERS

    A little context goes a long way.

    01 / MONTHLY PAYMENT

    A regular commitment.

    Your payment includes principal and interest. A longer term usually lowers the monthly amount while increasing total interest.

    02 / TOTAL INTEREST

    The cost of borrowing.

    Interest is calculated on the remaining balance each month. Compare total interest alongside the monthly payment.

    03 / REPAYMENT SCHEDULE

    A transparent path to zero.

    See how each payment reduces your balance. Rounding can make the final payment smaller than the regular amount.
